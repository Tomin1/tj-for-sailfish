Tänään jäljellä calculator for Sailfish OS
==========================================
This app calculates the number of days left in military service in Finland.

***I'm looking for a maintainer for this app. If you are interested, please email me.***

Questions and answers
---------------------
Here you may find some answers to questions asked from me.

### Why is this app not available in English, Swedish or some other language?
This app was created for Finnish conscripts speaking Finnish and I do not know the terms used in the app in English or Swedish.
However, contributions are welcome!

### Why is some of the code in Finnish? Wouldn't it be better to write it completely in English?
Again, I haven't figured out those terms in English so it was much easier to keep using Finnish for them.
Of course, English is more widely understood language and Qt uses it,
thus it would be nice to have it consistently in single language (English).
Anyway, contributions are welcome!

Building your own version
-------------------------
You can build your own version of the package with [Sailfish SDK](https://sailfishos.org/wiki/Sailfish_SDK).
